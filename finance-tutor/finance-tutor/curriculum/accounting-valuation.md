# Track A: Accounting and valuation

Draft outline. The curriculum-designer adjusts order and depth after the diagnostic, skipping anything already SECURE.

## Part 1: Reading accounts
1. The three statements and how they link
2. Accrual accounting and revenue recognition
3. The balance sheet in depth: assets, liabilities, equity, working capital
4. The cash flow statement, free cash flow and owner earnings
5. Profitability and returns: margins, ROE, ROCE, ROIC
6. Debt, leverage and liquidity
7. Share count, dilution, options and buybacks
8. Accounting red flags and earnings quality

## Part 2: Sector-specific accounts
9. Banks: loans, deposits, provisions, net interest margin, capital ratios, book value
10. Miners and royalty companies: reserves vs resources, AISC, NAV, P/NAV
11. Energy producers: reserves, decline rates, netbacks, hedging
12. Asset-light and platform businesses

## Part 3: Valuation
13. Time value of money and discount rates
14. Discounted cash flow, step by step
15. Multiples: P/E, EV/EBIT, EV/EBITDA, P/B — when each fits
16. Asset-based and NAV valuation
17. Margin of safety and scenario ranges

## Part 4: Judging businesses
18. Competitive advantage (moats)
19. Capital allocation: buybacks, dividends, acquisitions, reinvestment
20. Management quality and incentives
21. Country, currency and governance risk (frontier and emerging markets)
22. Putting it together: a full company analysis
