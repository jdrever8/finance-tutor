# Approved sources

The fact-checker checks lessons and questions against these. The owner approves any additions.

## Track B: UK personal finance (official, use first)
- gov.uk — tax, ISAs, pensions, state pension, inheritance tax
- HMRC rates and allowances pages (gov.uk) — current tax year figures
- Scottish Government / Revenue Scotland — Scottish income tax bands
- NHS Business Services Authority (NHS Pensions) — NHS Pension Scheme rules
- MoneyHelper (moneyhelper.org.uk) — government-backed guidance
- FCA (fca.org.uk) — regulation, consumer protections

## Track A: Accounting and valuation
- Company annual reports and filings (SEC EDGAR, Companies House, company investor relations sites)
- IFRS Foundation (ifrs.org) — accounting standards summaries
- Aswath Damodaran's NYU materials (pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar) — valuation methods and data
