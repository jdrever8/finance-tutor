# ISA investing and retiring at 50–55

Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: DRAFT (fact-checker: partly verified — see bottom)
Inputs: pensions-specialist, investments-specialist, second-opinion (AGREE WITH CHANGES), fact-checker.

## Question
How should the ~£32k ISA (mostly in a money market fund) be invested, and how does that fit a plan to taper down from ~50 and largely stop work ~55?

## Short answer
The plan is achievable. The NHS pension does the heavy lifting from 57–60 onwards, and the ISA is the bridge until then. But the ISA only gets big enough if it is actually invested. Cash at ~1% real won't do it.

## Key numbers (today's money; assumptions, not forecasts)
- NHS Pension Scheme (Scotland) 2015. Normal Pension Age = State Pension Age = **68** (born 1993, current law — CONFIRMED).
- Protected minimum pension age **55** for the NHS pension (joined before 4 Nov 2021 — CONFIRMED). The SIPP is likely **57** (ask the provider).
- Early retirement keeps roughly **56% at 55, 60% at 57, 68% at 60** of the pension (England & Wales factors; Scottish factors UNVERIFIED).
- Projected NHS pension: ~£27–29k at 68 if working to 55. Taken at 55 ≈ £15–16k; at 57 ≈ £16.5–17k; at 60 ≈ £18.5–19.5k. Tapering to 0.6 WTE from 50 costs only ~£1.4k/yr at 68 (career average).
- Pension built so far: estimate £6–7.5k/yr. **The Total Reward Statement overrides this.**
- State Pension 2026/27: £241.30/week (~£12.5k/yr) with 35 qualifying years (CONFIRMED via secondary sources). Stopping at 55 may leave ~32 years — check the forecast.
- Bridge needed if stopping at 55, spending **£25k/yr (ASSUMPTION — to confirm; also test £30k)**: ~£125k (NHS from 55) to ~£175k (NHS from 60).
- ISA at 55 with £650/month: ~£230k at 1% real (cash-like) vs ~£340k at 4% real. That's a **~£110k difference** from being invested. Lower if contributions drop with children.

## Recommendation
1. **Keep a buffer first.** Leave ~£10–15k in the money market fund until you've moved in (renovation overrun, baby, and a £4k emergency fund that will only be 1–2 months once you're living there). Borrowing is hard at ~100% LTV.
2. **Invest the rest (~£17–22k) in one global all-world tracker**, accumulation class, in ~6 monthly chunks (you've not held shares through a fall yet). Point the £650/month there from now.
   - %-fee platform: HSBC FTSE All-World Index C Acc (OCF 0.13% — CONFIRMED)
   - Flat-fee platform: Invesco FTSE All-World UCITS ETF Acc, FWRA (TER 0.15% — CONFIRMED, secondary sources)
3. **Gold:** change from 1 SGLN share a week (~£63/share in Sept 2026 = ~£3.3k/yr, roughly 40% of new money) to a fixed ~£30–50/month. Cap gold at 5–10% of the ISA. SILJ in the SIPP: keep as a small punt, don't add.
4. **Write the crash rule now:** "In a 30% fall I keep the £650/month going and sell nothing." Rebalance once a year.
5. **Long-term shape:** ~90% global equity / ≤10% gold. From ~45, build 2–3 years of bridge spending in cash or short gilts.
6. **SIPP:** not a priority now (building the buffer comes first). Revisit after the move-in. Some SIPP saving can fund the 57+ part of the bridge using an unused personal allowance (21% relief in, 25% tax-free, rest at 0–20% out). Switch strongly to the SIPP if your pay reaches the 42% band.
7. **NHS:** stay in. Taper hours from ~50. Use partial retirement from 55 (take ≥20%, pay cut ≥10%). Plan to draw the full pension around 57–60. ERRBO: no (only helps from 65; review ~40). Additional Pension: get an SPPA quote later — it's a top-up, not the bridge.

## What would change this
Real spending above £25k; children cutting contributions for longer; SPA rising further; the SIPP keeping a protected age of 55; Scottish factors differing; pay moving into the 42% band; getting married (survivor benefits, inheritance).

## Household (from second-opinion)
Wills before the baby (unmarried in Scotland). Check the house title. Partner: thin pension, no sick pay — income protection; Child Benefit claimed in her name for NI credits while part-time; check NHS partner's pension conditions for cohabitants.

## Update 2026-10-13 — platform is IBKR, budget confident
- Buffer reduced: keep ~£5k in the money market fund until move-in (baby and emergency top-up); invest ~£27k in ~6 monthly chunks.
- Use an ETF, as the HSBC fund is unlikely to be available on IBKR: **Invesco FTSE All-World Acc (FWRA)**, buying the GBP-quoted London line to avoid FX charges. Alternative: Vanguard FTSE All-World Acc (VWRP).
- IBKR UK ISA costs (UNVERIFIED, from third-party reviews): ~£3 per UK ETF trade up to £6k; ~£3/month minimum activity fee that commissions count towards. **Fractional shares are not available in IBKR ISAs** (IBKR's own page), so buy whole shares.
- So: **one buy a month** (FWRA with the £650; the leftover cash rolls over). **Stop the weekly SGLN buy**: £3 on a ~£63 share is ~5% per purchase, ~£150/yr in fees. Top up gold once or twice a year instead, if it's below the cap.
- Why a world tracker as the core: see the chat answer of 2026-10-13. Optional satellite (≤10–20%) for any stock-picking interest.
