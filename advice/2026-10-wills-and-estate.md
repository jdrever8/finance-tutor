# Wills and estate (Scotland, unmarried couple)

Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: DRAFT (from home-and-protection-specialist; official sites could not be fetched directly, so starred items need re-checking)

## Wishes
Everything to each other; if both die, to any children.

## Position now (no will)
- Unmarried partner = cohabitant: no automatic inheritance and no legal rights. Only a discretionary court claim (Family Law (Scotland) Act 2006 s29) within **6 months** of death (12 months under the 2024 Act once in force*).
- With no will: the estate goes to the owner's parents and siblings; once there are children, to the children.
- The title is in both names. If it has a **survivorship destination**, the deceased's share passes automatically to the survivor, overriding the will. Unknown — check the title sheet (Registers of Scotland via ScotLIS, ~£3 + VAT).

## Passes outside the will
- NHS (Scotland) death-in-service lump sum (~2× pensionable pay ≈ £80k) to a nominated/qualifying partner; survivor's pension if she meets the "scheme partner" test (2+ years living together, financially interdependent, free to marry)*. The nomination is done.
- SIPP: expression of wish with the provider.
- Life policies: **only bypass the estate if written in trust**. Otherwise they go through the estate (delay, intestacy, IHT).

## Children
- Legal rights: children can claim half the net **moveable** estate (cash, ISA — not the house) even if the will leaves everything to the partner*.
- Wills should appoint guardians once there are children.

## Inheritance tax
No spouse exemption for cohabitants, but the estate is well under the £325k nil-rate band (frozen to 2031*). Low risk now. Revisit after the renovation and as the ISA grows (the house + ISA could pass £325k in time). Unused pension funds come into IHT from April 2027 (death-in-service excluded)*.
